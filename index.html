Four checks before any reply goes out

No flows to build. Upload your product sheet and policy, and ShelfTalk does the rest.

  1. Reads the message

    Understands free-text questions, typos and short forms included.

  2. Checks your data

    Looks up products, live stock and policy. Never answers from memory.

  3. Writes the reply

    A friendly answer built only from what it found in your data.

  4. Verifies every number

    A price or stock figure not in your data never reaches the customer.
